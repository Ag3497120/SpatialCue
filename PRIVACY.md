# SpatialCue Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

SpatialCue does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.

## What stays on your devices
- **Motion data** from your Apple Watch and AirPods head tracking is processed in real time on the Watch and iPhone to recognize gestures. It is not saved, except for gesture samples you choose to record in **Training**, which stay on your Watch.
- **Settings** are stored on your iPhone and Watch and synced directly between them with WatchConnectivity.
- **Training data exports** (CSV) are sent from your Watch to your iPhone only when you tap **Export to iPhone**. You decide whether to share them anywhere else.

## Permissions
- **Motion & Fitness / Headphone Motion**: used to read wrist and AirPods head motion for gestures.
- **Apple Music / Media Library**: used only to play, pause, and skip tracks in the Music app when you ask.
- **Health (workout mode, optional)**: used only to start a workout session that keeps the Watch app running. No health data is read, and no workouts are saved.
